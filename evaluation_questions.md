**Evaluation Questions**



These questions are used to manually test retrieval quality and answer grounding for the SEC 10-K Intelligence Assistant.



&#x20;**Risk Factors**

\- What are the company's main risk factors?

\- What are the top 5 risks mentioned in the filing?



&#x20;**Business**

\- Summarize the company's business model in simple terms.

\- What products, services, or operating segments are described?



&#x20;**Cybersecurity**

\- What does the company say about cybersecurity?

\- What data security or information security risks are mentioned?



&#x20;**Competition**

\- What does the company say about competition?

\- What competitive risks are described?



&#x20;**Legal / Regulatory**

\- What are the major legal and regulatory risks?

\- What compliance or litigation risks are mentioned?



&#x20;**Financial Risks**

\- What financial risks are described in the filing?

\- What does the company say about liquidity, revenue, cash flow, or market risk?
