Project Name:
SEC 10-K Intelligence Assistant

Project Summary:
Built a RAG-based financial document intelligence app using public SEC EDGAR 10-K filings. The system downloads a company’s latest 10-K, parses and chunks the filing text, generates OpenAI embeddings, stores vectors in ChromaDB, retrieves relevant document chunks, and generates source-grounded answers through a Streamlit interface.

Tools Used:
Python, Streamlit, ChromaDB, OpenAI Embeddings, OpenAI Chat Model, SEC EDGAR API, BeautifulSoup, dotenv

Tested Questions:
1. What are the company's main risk factors?
2. What are the company's top 5 risk factors?
3. Summarize the business model in simple terms.
4. What does the company say about cybersecurity?
5. What are the major legal and regulatory risks?
6. What does the company say about competition?

Current Status:
MVP successfully built and tested locally. Screenshots saved for portfolio documentation.
